## Safeguards

- Fuller is comparison, not liberator-vs-thief.
- Prototype ≠ adopted production system.
- Software last — after students can separate contribution, ownership, attribution, and market control.
- No Fuller quotation from memory: every Fuller term stays a course prompt until a page is checked.

## C1–C3 hooks

- **C1 ideology:** “comprehensive design” can hide who is left outside the system.
- **C2 labour:** factory workers and maintenance as invisible authorship.
- **C3 GenAI authorship:** software transfer — licensing, branding, and platform governance ≠ technical invention; ask who is named when a model writes code.
