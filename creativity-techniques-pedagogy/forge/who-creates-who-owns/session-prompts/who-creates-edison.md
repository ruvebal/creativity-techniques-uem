## Safeguards

- Five cases are attribution investigations, not five thefts.
- Phonograph: protect Edison’s contribution while naming Kruesi / Cros / Scott (dossier only — no Chicago).
- Radio: not an Edison invention claim.
- MPPC: market organisation / access control via patents and standards.
- Appropriation ≠ legal acquisition ≠ collaboration ≠ infringement ≠ monopolization.
- Do not read dossier footnotes or discovery-only coats as citations.

## C1–C3 hooks

- **C1 ideology:** whose interests the genius story serves — hagiographic biographies omitted family life (Robb 2024, 242); Mina Edison “helped cultivate Edison’s public image as a genius” (Robb 2024, 243). The press coining “Wizard of Menlo Park” by 1878 is a discovery-only lead (Jeffrey, `426a6072`) — say it without a page.
- **C2 labour:** laboratory assistants, machinists, the Insomnia Squad, Clarence Dally (discovery only); factory film (LOC *Day with Edison*, rights unchecked).
- **C3 GenAI authorship:** exit — how would a model-generated “inventor story” flatten the five evidence categories?

## Analysis triad on product stories

Language (claims, patents, branding, “Edison Junior Improved”) · medium (exhibition, press, peephole vs projection) · support (wiring, foil, film stock).
