# Session prompt — Discursive circulation (masterclass)

> Unpublished professor surface. Student deck ≤ 6 ideas via forge packing.
> Contract: [`DISCURSIVE-CIRCULATION-FORGE.mdc`](../DISCURSIVE-CIRCULATION-FORGE.mdc)

## Stake

Creativity students must treat studio outputs as **communicative artefacts in
motion**. Meaning is generated in **recognition**, not sealed in production.
Forms matter as **forms in circulation** (palimpsests under a brief) — not as
timeless shapes.

## Learning outcomes

1. Distinguish **lenguaje / medio / soporte** without collapsing them.
2. Describe **puesta en circulación discursiva** for one technique + artefact.
3. Locate a **desajuste** between production and recognition.
4. Name a provisional **contrato de lectura** for a pitch or campaign piece.
5. Classify an artefact on triad + circulation + technique-job axes.

## ≤ 6 slideshow ideas (draft)

1. **Meaning arrives in reception** — Production proposes; recognition decides.
2. **Language ≠ medium ≠ support** — Code, channel, and substrate are three jobs.
3. **Put it into circulation** — Techniques matter when they enter social reading.
4. **The gap is normal** — Desajuste is structural, not student failure.
5. **Reading contracts** — Tone and trust are part of the creative brief.
6. **Palimpsest under deadline** — Iteration overwrites; keep the process trail.
