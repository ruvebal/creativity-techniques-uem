## Archived classroom text (verbatim as drafted)

### Exercise 3 — Creative Campus tech tools

Practises Masterclass ideas 1 (stage the tool), 3 (refuse originality theatre), and 6 (name the costs).

**Time:** 20 minutes (classroom adaptation).  
**Group:** pairs; photographer and scribe.  
**Materials:** phone or camera; notebook; shared class brief or D1 problem; optional generative tool only after the tour.

**Steps:**

1. Tour authorised Creative Campus studios and tech zones you already may enter. Photograph three tools or machines (screens, printers, cameras, cutting equipment, audio desks — tools, not people). Delete any frame that shows a face or badge.
2. For each tool, write: what it does; one constraint it forces (time, noise, booking, skill); one line on who maintains it or bears its cost if you know.
3. Pick one tool. Write a title and one-line label that places it in a new context for your brief (readymade move — change context, not the object).
4. Optional: generate one early variant with a generative tool that answers the same brief, then name what the campus tool can do that the generated surface cannot (and the reverse).
5. Seated alternative: from memory or a campus map, list three tools in this building and complete steps 2–4 without new photographs.

**Portfolio trace:** three tool photos or sketches; constraint notes; one recontextualised label; optional GenAI comparison sentence; consent note (no people).

**Claimed technique ids:** `seed-collection`, `swipe-file`, `readymade-recontextualisation`
