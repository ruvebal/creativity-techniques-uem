## 6. Candidate main ideas (draft; may exceed 6)

1. Separate diverge (generate) from converge (select) in time.
2. Fluency without flexibility is one idea in twenty costumes.
3. Inhibit obvious first answers — executive control in DT tasks.
4. Role-separated hats / constraint cards as group and solo gestures.
5. Selection criteria must be stated (novelty + appropriateness).
6. Brainstorming is training theatre unless selection is designed.
