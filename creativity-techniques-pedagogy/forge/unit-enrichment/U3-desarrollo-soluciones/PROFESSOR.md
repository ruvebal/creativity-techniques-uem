## 6. Candidate main ideas (draft; may exceed 6)

1. Rough prototypes invite reaction before investment.
2. Sacrificial / crazy concepts protect exploration.
3. Revision is a skill type — know when to stop.
4. Sketching and visual framing are thinking, not decoration.
5. Abductive moves open solution space under wicked briefs.
6. Development = reflective checkpoints, not endless polish.
