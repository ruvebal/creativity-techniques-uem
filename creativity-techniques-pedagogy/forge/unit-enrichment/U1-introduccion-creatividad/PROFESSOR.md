## 6. Candidate main ideas (draft; may exceed 6)

1. Creativity is paced as diverge then converge — not a mystical gift.
2. Fluency, flexibility, originality, elaboration name assessable craft dimensions.
3. Context matters: brick tests ≠ design talent (Chen caution).
4. Wicked briefs need framing before techniques (Buchanan).
5. Studio learning is reflection-in-action (Schön), not checklist execution.
6. Design thinking is a contested discourse (Kimbell), not a single method.
7. Teachable creativity vs talent myth — course stance.
