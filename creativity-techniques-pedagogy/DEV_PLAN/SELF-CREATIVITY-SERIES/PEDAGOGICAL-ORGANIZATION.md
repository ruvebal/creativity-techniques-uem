## Arguments students should leave with

1. Attention style is a **designable** parameter of ideation.  
2. Divergent and convergent are **different jobs** — schedule them (U2 continuity).  
3. Meditation is **not** one thing; OM ≠ FA.  
4. Personal creativity lives in **habits and flow**, not mood alone.  
5. Shared images (Jung) and institutions (Castoriadis) shape what feels “original.”  
6. Transcendental / imaginal languages (Plotinus, Corbin) are **cultural tools**, not compulsory belief.  
7. Every classroom dose that differs from the study must be labelled **adaptation**.
