## Measurement protocol and consent (drafts, private)

- **Design:** one-group pre/post, week 1 (session 1, before the U1 Lab) and week 14; no causal claim; task-practice threat stated (the U1 Lab practises an AUT).
- **AUT:** two parallel forms (A: tin can, sock; B: cardboard box, umbrella — none used in class), 3 minutes per object, counterbalanced by code.
- **Peer CAT:** described procedurally — independent, silent, blinded (pre and post mixed, after week 14), ≥ 5 peer raters per set, never one's own, 1–5 relative to the set, reliability ≥ 0.70 before averaging. **Amabile 1982 is a gap: source pending**, not cited.
- **Short Scale of Creative Self (Karwowski 2012): gap, source pending** — items, scale and scoring not reproduced; study runs AUT-only if not obtained.
- **Anonymisation:** self-generated code, no names/IDs, consent handled by a third person so the grading professor does not know who took part, paper destroyed after transcription, data only in university storage (never in the repo, never processed by a model), groups < 5 not reported.
- **Analysis plan:** primary = change in CAT originality (paired t or Wilcoxon, mean difference + 95% CI + d_z); secondary fluency, flexibility, SSCS with Holm correction; fluency-confound and form-order sensitivity checks; attrition reported.
- **Ethics route:** university research ethics committee before any collection (even internal use), data-protection officer, approval reference on consent forms and `APPROVAL.md`.
- **Consent:** Spanish and English, same content: voluntary, no effect on marks, what participants do, withdrawal by code until a date, GDPR/Spanish law basis, placeholders for the reference, third person, retention and DPO.
