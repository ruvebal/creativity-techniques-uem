BLOOM: analyse
STEM: How does the "generate, then select" method improve the creative process?
ANSWER: It separates the generation of ideas from the selection process, preventing early judgement and allowing for a wider range of ideas to be considered.
DISTRACTORS: 
EVIDENCE: Generation and selection are two different jobs; doing them in the same minute makes both weaker.
