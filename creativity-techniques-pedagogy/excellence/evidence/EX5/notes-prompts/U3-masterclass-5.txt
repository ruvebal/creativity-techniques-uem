### 5 · Open the solution space

Some briefs are unclear because the problem itself is still moving. Do not force one path too soon. Reframe the brief as two or three possible questions, then make one small response to each. This is a bounded way to move from creative potential to creative action: Beghetto and Karwowski define creative agency as the capacity to envision and enact meaningful changes within constraints [<a href="#ref-beghetto-karwowski-2025">Beghetto and Karwowski 2025, 1</a>].
