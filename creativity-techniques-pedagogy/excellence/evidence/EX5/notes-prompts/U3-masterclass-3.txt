### 3 · Iterate with an exit

Revision becomes useful when you know what the next version must answer. Write one test before you change the work: “Can the viewer find the main action?” or “Does the form fit the brief?” Stop when the test is answered, even if another improvement is possible. Development needs direction as well as patience.
