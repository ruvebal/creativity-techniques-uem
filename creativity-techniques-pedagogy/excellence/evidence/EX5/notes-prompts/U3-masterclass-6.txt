LESSON:
### 6 · Reflect at checkpoints

Pause after each version and write three lines: what changed, what stayed, and what the change taught you. Schön’s reflection-in-action tradition gives this pause a useful frame, while Cross’s designerly knowing keeps attention on how the work is made and tested. The checkpoint turns a sequence of attempts into evidence you can discuss; it is a teaching prompt, not a claim that one model explains every studio decision.
