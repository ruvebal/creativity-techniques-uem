3: For a signage project, you might start with obvious ideas like arrows and text, but push further to explore unique symbols and forms to avoid settling too quickly.

4: In an app screen design workshop, using prompt cards to guide each participant can lead to a wider range of ideas than if one person led the discussion.

5: Before selecting a product design, you might agree on criteria like user-friendliness or sustainability, ensuring everyone knows what makes a design successful.

6: After a workshop on exhibition design, you might have more ideas, but not all are better; evaluate each based on how well it meets the exhibition's goals.

10: When designing an app screen, you might sketch three different layouts to explore various ways the content could be arranged, rather than perfecting one.

11: For a poster brief, you might reframe the vague idea of "promoting a festival" into specific questions like "how can we show the festival's theme?" and "how can we highlight the lineup?"
