## What is this study?

We want to know whether two things change between the first and the last week
of this course: how you generate ideas in a short timed task, and how you see
yourself as a creative person. The results help evaluate a teaching innovation
in this course. They cannot show that the course *caused* any change.

## What would I do?

- **Week 1 (first session) and week 14 (last session), about 20 minutes each:**
  list as many uses as you can for two everyday objects (3 minutes per object),
  and, if the questionnaire is ready, answer a short questionnaire about how you
  see yourself as a creative person.
- **After the term, optional:** rate other students' anonymous answers on a
  printed sheet. You never rate your own answers.

## Is it compulsory? Does it affect my mark?

No. Taking part is voluntary. Saying no, or stopping at any time, has **no
effect on any mark** in this or any other course. The tasks are never graded.
Everyone in the class does the same timed task. If you do not consent, your
sheet is destroyed unread by [third person] and is not part of the study.
The lecturer cannot tell who took part by watching the class, and does not
know who took part until final marks are published: this form is collected
by [third person], not by the lecturer.

## How is my information protected?

- You write a **code that you make up yourself** (instructions on the answer
  sheet), never your name or student number. Nobody keeps a list linking codes
  to names.
- Paper sheets are typed up, checked and then destroyed. The typed data are kept
  only in university-approved storage, for [retention period], and are never
  published.
- Results are reported only as group totals; no group smaller than five people
  is shown.
- Your data are processed on the basis of your consent, under the EU General
  Data Protection Regulation and Spanish data-protection law. You can ask the
  data-protection contact above about your rights.

## Can I withdraw?

Yes. Give your code to [third person] by [date when codes are dropped] and your
answers are deleted. After that date the data can no longer be linked to anyone,
so they cannot be removed.

## Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond the time spent (about 40 minutes in total, plus
optional rating). There is no direct benefit to you; you may find the tasks a
useful way to see your own progress.
