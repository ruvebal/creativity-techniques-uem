## No-repeat law across lessons

Before a Lab is accepted, compare its title, catalogue URN, canonical technique IDs, and actual steps against every earlier student-facing Lab. Do not assign an exact or functionally equivalent exercise twice, including when the title, brief, grouping, or materials change. A later Lab may use an earlier portfolio output only when it performs a materially different operation. Repeated theory is allowed; repeated exercises are not. Record the audit and reject any duplicate candidate in the private forge receipt and gated lesson selection block. Choose a distinct catalogue-backed candidate or report the gap.
