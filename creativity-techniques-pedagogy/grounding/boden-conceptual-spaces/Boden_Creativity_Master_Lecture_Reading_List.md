## Companion Approaches for Classroom Comparison

| Author | Canonical Work | Year | Approach Label | Core Claim | Design / HE Relevance |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Teresa M. Amabile** | *Creativity in Context* | 1996 | Componential model | Creativity emerges from domain expertise, intrinsic motivation, and creative process skills. | Excellent bridge to studio assessment and feedback. |
| **Teresa M. Amabile** | Consensual Assessment Technique (CAT) papers | 1982 onward | Assessment model | Creativity can be reliably assessed through expert judgment within a domain. | Direct applicability to design juries and portfolio evaluation. |
| **Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi** | *Creativity: Flow and the Psychology of Discovery and Invention* | 1996 | Systems model | Creativity is produced by interactions among person, field, and domain. | Particularly useful in fashion and design ecosystems. |
| **Anna Craft** | *Creativity in Schools* | 2005 | Possibility thinking | Educational creativity arises through question-posing and possibility exploration. | Highly relevant to design pedagogy. |
| **Maria Kronfeldner** | *What's Left of Human Nature?* and creativity papers | 2009–2018 | Philosophical explanation | Creativity should be explained through evolving biological, social and historical mechanisms. | Useful critique of individualist theories. |
| **Katherine Hawley** | "Creativity and Knowledge" | 2018 | Epistemic approach | Creativity depends on knowledge structures rather than opposing them. | Good corrective to creativity-versus-expertise myths. |
| **Vlad Glăveanu** | *Distributed Creativity* | 2014 | Sociocultural model | Creativity is distributed across people, artifacts and contexts. | Very strong for collaborative design practice. |
| **James Kaufman & Ronald Beghetto** | "Beyond Big and Little" (4C model) | 2009 | Developmental model | Creativity exists across mini-c, little-c, Pro-c and Big-C levels. | Useful for student progression and curriculum design. |
| **Mark Runco & Garrett Jaeger** | "The Standard Definition of Creativity" | 2012 | Novelty-value model | Creativity requires both novelty and effectiveness/value. | Ideal opening reading for master's students. |
