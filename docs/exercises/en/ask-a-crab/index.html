Exercises · U4

Ask a Crab — picture diverge

Use an unrelated picture to open workplace ideas before you judge. Save the path from image to idea — not only the favourite.

  • ~20 min
  • same team as the U4 idea set
  • technique brainstorming-osborn
  • unit U4

Source book Michalko, Michael. 2010. Thinkertoys: A Handbook of Creative-Thinking Techniques. 2nd ed. Berkeley: Ten Speed Press.

On this page

  • Requirements
  • Steps
  • Source
  • Portfolio

Requirements

Time ~20 minutes
Group same team as the U4 idea set
Access Large-print or screen images; oral idea lists OK.

Materials

  • unrelated picture prompt
  • U4 brief or workplace problem
  • paper

Before you start

  • Keep generation separate from selection.
  • Record the picture ID or description in the trace.

Steps

Follow in order — your professor may shorten the clock.

  1. Show one unrelated picture to the team.
  2. In a timed pass, each person writes ideas the picture suggests for the workplace brief.
  3. Read ideas aloud without ranking them yet.
  4. Save a short path: picture → association → idea.
  5. Stop before voting; selection belongs to a later exercise.

Source

Give each group member a folder containing two or three pictures that are not related to the problem area. Read aloud a problem statement and ask the group to verbally brainstorm solutions. Instruct the group members to examine each picture and write their ideas. After a designated period, ask the group members to read their ideas aloud. As each idea is read, ask the group members to discuss it and try to develop new ideas or modifications. Record all new ideas as they are suggested.

— (Michalko 2010, 440)

Classroom note. Classroom adaptation of Ask a Crab’s early blueprint steps; full five-step source is not claimed as the class procedure.

Portfolio

Picture note; idea list; one association path; no selection claim.

Judged by: Portfolio rubric — creative fluency, flexibility, and originality
