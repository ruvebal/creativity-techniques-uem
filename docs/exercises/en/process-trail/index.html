Requirements

Time ~20 minutes
Group individual; optional pair read
Access Digital board accepted; voice caption for the turning point OK.

Materials

  • exports from a recent making loop
  • one board slide or A3 sheet

Before you start

  • Include discarded options.
  • Attach tool prompts where they belong.

Steps

Follow in order — your professor may shorten the clock.

  1. Save every version, sketch, and discarded idea with a date or order number.
  2. Arrange them in order on one page or board.
  3. Mark where you diverged (opened options) and converged (closed them).
  4. Write one paragraph on the turning point — what changed your direction?
  5. Attach tool prompts to the steps where they belong.

Source

A process trail makes the path of work legible: every sketch, discarded option, and revision is kept in order so that diverge and converge moves can be marked, and so that a later reader can find the turning point where judgment changed the direction of the work. In this course the trail is the assessable object—not a single polished final—because creativity techniques are judged by how options were opened and closed under a brief.
