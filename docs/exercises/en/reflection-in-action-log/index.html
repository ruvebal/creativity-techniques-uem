Reflection-in-action log

Log surprises while you work — when the situation talks back — then name one move you changed.

  • ~15 min
  • individual
  • technique reflection-in-action-log
  • unit U3
  • unit U6

Source book Schön, Donald A. 1983. The Reflective Practitioner: How Professionals Think in Action. New York: Basic Books.

On this page

  • Requirements
  • Steps
  • Source
  • Portfolio

Requirements

Time ~15 minutes
Group individual
Access Voice notes accepted; no drawing required.

Materials

  • current making session
  • notebook or voice notes

Before you start

  • Log during work, not only after polishing.
  • Keep entries short and dated.

Steps

Follow in order — your professor may shorten the clock.

  1. During a short making pass, note three moments of surprise or resistance.
  2. For each, write what the work “said back” (a constraint, a failure, an unexpected fit).
  3. Name one strategy you changed in response.
  4. Mark whether the change opened or closed options.
  5. Keep the log beside the artefact for critique.

Source

In a good process of design, this conversation with the situation is reflective. In answer to the situation’s back-talk, the designer reflects-in-action on the construction of the problem, the strategies of action, or the model of the phenomena, which have been implicit in his moves. […] But the practitioner’s moves also produce unintended changes which give the situations new meanings. The situation talks back, the practitioner listens; and as he appreciates what he hears, he reframes the situation once again. When Quist discovers that his moves have produced a gallery which is “in a minor way . . . the major thing,” he becomes aware of a new whole idea, which sets criteria for the further designing.

— (Schön 1983, 79)

Classroom note. Classroom timing and portfolio fields adapt Schön’s reflective conversation to a short studio log.

Portfolio

Three surprise notes; one changed strategy; open/close mark.

Judged by: Portfolio rubric — critical analysis and judgement
