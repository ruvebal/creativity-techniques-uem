# U4 · Applying creativity techniques in the workplace

Official CONTENIDOS: **Aplicación de las técnicas de creatividad en el ámbito laboral.** This unit carries idea generation and selection into briefs, deadlines, teams, and professional responsibility.

## Two lessons

1. [U4.1 · Generate before you judge]({{ '/lessons/en/creativity-techniques/u-4-1-generate-before-you-judge/' | relative_url }}) — use an unrelated image to open new routes through a workplace brief.
2. [U4.2 · Follow the visitor, then choose what to check]({{ '/lessons/en/creativity-techniques/u-4-2-select-with-care/' | relative_url }}) — observe a route, select an idea that answers an observed moment, and name what still needs checking.

Both lessons use the [U4 in-class deck]({{ '/tracks/ct/u-4-workplace-application/' | relative_url }}). The second Lab exercise continues the first lesson’s saved idea set.
