## Learning objectives

1. Treat **creative confidence** as practiced evidence under briefs (U6 bridge).
2. Name **Can I? / Should I?** as agency gates.
3. Host or refuse a **shared image** without therapy theatre.
4. Audit **instituted categories** in a brief (Castoriadis as studio method).

## Analysis

Ask what the maker **practiced** — and which collective image they borrowed or broke. Still use language / medium / support.

## Masterclass — six ideas

### 1 · Confidence is practiced

Creative confidence grows from doing — not from waiting to feel gifted (U6 spine; Kelley lineage as course paraphrase).

### 2 · Can I? Should I?

Agency needs belief you **can** and that creativity **matters** to who you are (Beghetto & Karwowski lineage as course paraphrase).

### 3 · Habits outlast the high

Personal creativity redesigns everyday psychic energy — work, relations, attention under briefs [(Csikszentmihalyi 1996)](#ref-csikszentmihalyi-1996).

### 4 · Images larger than the ego

Some images arrive as if not only yours. We **name** Jung’s landmark formulation of a collective unconscious — we do **not** extract alchemy corpora or play therapist in class.

### 5 · Imaginal as mediation

The imaginal is a mediating function — neither raw fantasy nor mere sensation [(Corbin 1972, 7)](#ref-corbin-1972).

### 6 · Institutions invent worlds

Method is the operating set of categories [(Castoriadis 1975, 11)](#ref-castoriadis-1975). Design participates in what a society institutes as real. Plotinus: optional **flagship mention** of the *Enneads* / *Plotini Opera* — no monograph dive.

## Lab

### Exercise 1 — Shared-image note

Name one image in your brief larger than you (brand, myth, meme, style). Host or refuse it in one sentence.

### Exercise 2 — Category audit

List three categories the brief assumes; star one you will revise.
