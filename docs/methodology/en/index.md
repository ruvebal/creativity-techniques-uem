## Why a methodology of its own

**Creativity Techniques** (UEM Creative Campus) teaches **method literacy for design practice** — how to generate, select, develop, and defend ideas under real constraints (briefs, deadlines, competition). This course owns the *technique* and the *judgement* that decides what survives.

Shared studio motto: **AI exists to amplify human intention, not to replace it.**

Every unit maps to the official course contents, and every claim of “technique” must show process evidence.

## Studio cycle

Exploration → Reflection → Conceptualization → Production → Critique.

| Phase | Student does | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploration | Apply divergent techniques to a constrained brief | Stimulus log |
| Reflection | Name what was discarded and why | Selection criteria note |
| Conceptualization | Converge on a defensible concept | Concept sheet |
| Production | Develop the solution with craft appropriate to the brief | Artefact + source trail |
| Critique | Present, receive, revise | Presentation + revision note |

## Official methods (guía)

From the official course guide (Design degree): **Magistral class**, **cooperative learning**, **experiential learning**.

## Visible authorship

Every substantial hand-in declares:

1. What the student made  
2. Which techniques were used (named, not vibes)  
3. What was discarded  
4. Whether AI assisted — tool, contribution, human verification
