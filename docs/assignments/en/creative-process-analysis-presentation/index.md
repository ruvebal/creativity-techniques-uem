## The assignment in one sentence

Analyse **one documented creative process** (not only the pretty final artefact) with the eight-step Master Lecture method, then **present that analysis live** so classmates can follow your evidence, feel why the process mattered, and question your judgements.

The deliverable is not a written essay handed in alone. The deliverable is a **presentation file** you can run in class: PowerPoint / Keynote / Google Slides, Acrobat PDF as slides, online Figma (presentation mode), or another slideshow format approved by the professor. You speak with the slides — the slides do not speak for you.

## Why we are doing this

Creativity Techniques trains method literacy under constraint. A silent process diary helps you; a **public defence** trains what studios and critiques require: to transmit ideas clearly, show enthusiasm for the work *and* for the hard calls, and survive questions without collapsing into autobiography or tool lists.

Two presentations run per Analysis session. Each slot is **maximum 15 minutes including questions and feedback**. Short time forces selection: which stage matters, which discard proves judgement, which critical question you will own aloud.

## What to analyse

Choose **one** process trail you can evidence (your Lab sequence, a named brief you ran, or a documented collaborative process where your role is clear). Show traces: sketches, discarded options, screenshots, timelines, constraint cards — not only the hero final.

Follow the Master Lecture card:

1. Describe → 2. Inventory → 3. Forms → 4. Origin → 5. Context of making → 6. Meaning (hypothesis + evidence) → 7. Critical → 8. Circulation (language / medium / support; production → recognition → next making).

**Without step 7 the defence fails.** A fluency story without labour, power, or authorship questions is incomplete. Hook C1–C3 when they fit (ideology of “creativity”; unpaid ideation; GenAI disclosure).

## What to submit (the presentation)

Submit **one** navigable slideshow package through Campus Virtual (format and date announced there), containing:

- **Title + process ID** (brief name, date range, your role).
- **Eight-step trail** — process traces on screen when you claim them.
- **Lens A** named at least once (where the technique acts: language / medium / support).
- **Lens B** named at least once (who recognised the work; what it could produce next).
- **One critical sentence** you will defend under questions.
- **Authorship / AI declaration** (tools, collaborators, “no AI” valid) for this presentation, following the [AI usage declaration]({{ '/ai-declaration/' | relative_url }}).
- **Speaker-ready file**: opens reliably; media linked; fonts safe.

Optional handout ≠ replacement for the live deck.

## Live defence rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Two presentations per Analysis session |
| **Time** | **≤ 15 minutes total** — talk + questions + feedback |
| **Suggested split** | ~8–10 min present · ~5–7 min Q&A / feedback |
| **Who speaks** | You. Slide-reading is a public-communication fail |
| **Audience duty** | Ask for process evidence; give one usable feedback line |

**Public communication** is graded: transmit ideas **and enthusiasm** — energy for the argument, respect for peers’ time, audible commitment without replacing critique with cheerleading.

## Rubric

The rubric uses a 0–10 score for this defence. Official guide weights on Campus Virtual remain authoritative; this table states how *this* presentation is judged.

| Criterion | Weight | 9–10 · Excellent | 7–8.9 · Competent | 5–6.9 · Minimum pass | 0–4.9 · Insufficient |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Analytic completeness (8 steps + lenses)** | 25% | Full trail; lenses explicit; critique earned from the process evidence. | Almost full; lenses named; critique present. | Missing steps; lenses collapsed; critique thin. | Final-only show-and-tell; method abandoned. |
| **Critical judgement & process evidence** | 25% | Shows discards, constraints, labour/authorship stakes; ties claims to traces. | Clear criteria; traces usually support claims. | Narrative without enough traces. | Tool tour, hero final, or no critical stake. |
| **Public communication & enthusiasm** | 25% | Ideas land; pacing fits 15′; audible commitment without hype replacing judgement. | Clear, audible, mostly on time; some presence. | Flat, rushed, or over-read. | Inaudible, overtime hostility, or slides replace speaking. |
| **Slideshow craft & authorship** | 15% | Hierarchy and traces serve the talk; declaration complete; file reliable. | Adequate slides; minor issues; declaration present. | Cluttered or fragile. | Broken deck or missing declaration/credits. |
| **Exchange quality (Q&A)** | 10% | Answers with process evidence; uses feedback; respects remaining time. | Relevant answers; accepts one critique. | Vague or defensive. | No engagement with questions. |
