Neoplastic hero lab

Instead of hexagons or triangles, this hero field is a perfect squared square: 21 squares, no two equal, filling a square of side 112 (Duijvestijn, 1978). Every ground plane can open into the whole. Three modes, one geometry, this course's colours.

Grounding

Mathematics, not Mondrian's proportions: Duijvestijn's tiling is the unique simple perfect squared square of lowest order (21). Colour planes never blend; they enter by orthogonal wipe. With reduced motion the still image stays.
